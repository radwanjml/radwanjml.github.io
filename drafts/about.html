About · field notes

Engineer by degree, world-builder by habit.

I'm Radwan, Red to most people. I model things in Blender and then make them live in the browser.

I studied electronics and communication engineering at Osmania University (2024), spent a few years in technical support for Amazon, Verizon and ADP, and kept building 3D on the side. In June 2026 I started Tibr Studio and began shipping interactive 3D sites in Three.js and custom GLSL.

What I care about: scenes that feel like places, motion driven by code instead of keyframes, and pages that still run on an ordinary phone.
