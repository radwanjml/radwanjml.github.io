Straight answers

What agencies ask first.

Why you, when others have more experience?

Don't hire me on trust. Order the paid test. You see the quality on your own client's product for the price of a lunch, and decide from that. You also get a lower rate than a senior studio, a reply within hours, and one person who does both the 3D and the web build, so nothing gets lost between a render studio and a developer.

Do you write the code yourself?

I model, light and animate by hand in Blender. The browser code is written with AI coding tools: I design how it works, direct the AI, review every change and test on real phones. Each page is held to a size budget, which is why SERAI is about 251 KB.

Will 3D slow the site down?

It can, which is why I build it light: real HTML content first, the 3D loaded after, and a still image on slow phones. Faster pages sell more. Vodafone measured 8% more sales from a 31% faster load, so speed is part of the job, not an extra.

Can our client know about you?

Only if you want them to. I work under your name and sign your NDA. I show the work publicly only with your written permission, after launch.

What about time zones?

I'm on India time. That covers your afternoon in Europe, most of the working day in the Gulf, and early mornings on the US east coast. You get a written update at the end of every working day.

What if it isn't right?

Two revision rounds are included, and the clay preview comes early so problems surface before the expensive part. If the paid test doesn't convince you, we stop there and you owe nothing more.
