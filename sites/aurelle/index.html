Common Questions

We get asked a lot

Will any of this hurt? +

Our philosophy is that no procedure should ever feel uncomfortable. We use the finest anaesthesia protocols and offer conscious sedation for those who prefer it. Most patients describe treatment as deeply relaxing.

How long does treatment take, start to finish? +

It depends on the treatment. Whitening can be completed in a single session. Veneers typically require two visits over a week or two. Full smile makeovers are planned across several appointments — we never rush craftsmanship.

What's the difference between veneers and whitening? +

Whitening brightens your natural tooth colour — ideal if you're happy with shape and alignment. Veneers replace the visible surface of the tooth entirely, allowing us to change colour, shape, and subtle imperfections simultaneously. Dr. Layla will recommend what's right for your goals, not what's most involved.

Do you cater to international patients? +

Yes, and it's something we specialise in. Our concierge team will coordinate your schedule across visits, assist with hotel recommendations, and ensure your aftercare is seamless — whether you're returning to Dubai or travelling home.

How do I know if I'm a good candidate? +

The best way to find out is a no-commitment consultation. We'll assess your dental health, discuss your goals, and be completely honest — including if a simpler option would serve you just as well.

How is payment handled? +

All investment is discussed transparently after your consultation, with no hidden fees. We accept all major cards and can discuss flexible arrangements where appropriate. A full treatment plan is provided before any commitment is made.
