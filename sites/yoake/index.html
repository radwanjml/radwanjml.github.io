I  /  門   THE GATE 01 — 04

A threshold is a change of air

The gate does not keep anyone out. It marks the line where the ordinary road ends and the path begins, and asks you to notice crossing it.

Two uprights, a curved lintel, and a second beam beneath it. Vermilion, because the colour was thought to hold the weather off the timber — and it does, for about a decade at a time.
