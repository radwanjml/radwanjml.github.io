The Letter of Offer is the document that counts

Everything on this page is indicative. Your written Letter of Offer sets out the fees that actually apply to you, and it is the authoritative schedule. Instalment amounts and dates are set out in your written agreement.

Fee schedules are period-specific. If your offer is issued in a later period, the figures in it are the ones that apply.

Four published sources, four different fee structures

The website, the individual course flyers, the “Onshore and Offshore Fees” flyer and the agent promotional flyer each publish a different set of figures for the same four qualifications. The gap between the highest and lowest published tuition for ICT60220 is $16,000.

This page uses the website schedule — the most recent, most granular and most specific of the four, and the only one that distinguishes onshore from offshore. The other three are still in circulation and should be withdrawn or reconciled.

Encyclopedia Part 4 · Register #1

Five published rows do not add up

Three of the four domestic ICT60220 rows state $18,500 tuition against a $15,000 total — the parts sum to $19,500. The fourth stream reads $14,000 tuition against $15,000, which reconciles, so $14,000 is almost certainly the intended figure and is what appears above. That is an inference, and it needs confirming.

Offshore BSB80120 states a $14,000 total against parts that sum to $14,500. Every total on this page is computed from its own tuition, material and application fee rather than copied from the published total column, which is why some differ from the current site by a few hundred dollars.

Encyclopedia §4.3 · Register #2

Instalment amounts are deliberately not shown

Two published payment plans do not sum to their own totals: the onshore ICT60220 plan is $100.50 short, and the offshore BSB80120 plan is $1,000 short. The instalment count is consistent across sources and is shown; the amounts stay behind the written agreement rather than being published wrong.

Published fee information for a CRICOS provider carries obligations under the ESOS framework. A prospective student who cannot compute a correct total from the published table is a compliance exposure, not a design problem.

What the number above does not include

Course fees are one line of the budget. These are the others, and they are the ones people are surprised by.

OSHC
Overseas Student Health Cover is mandatory for the length of your visa and is arranged separately. Costs vary by provider and cover period.
Student visa
The Department of Home Affairs charges its own application fee, which is not paid to AGIT.
Living in Perth
Accommodation, transport, food and phone. A Perth living-cost estimate is being prepared.
Your own equipment
A laptop, reliable internet, notebooks and a USB or external drive. IT students need additional storage and the ability to install software.
English testing
IELTS, PTE or TOEFL fees, if you do not already hold a result.
Document certification
Certified copies of your passport, transcripts and qualifications.

Refunds, scholarships and paying through an agent

The application fee

$250, non-refundable, payable when you accept your Letter of Offer. This figure is consistent across every AGIT source and is the one number on this page that has never been in doubt.

Refunds and the Tuition Protection Service

The refund policy and Tuition Protection Service statement are published separately.

Every CRICOS provider is required to have both, and to make them available to students before enrolment. Ask admissions for the current documents if you cannot find them.

Scholarships

AGIT’s course flyers refer to Foundational Cohort Scholarships for 2026, available for selected intakes, with eligibility criteria applying. Value and criteria on enquiry.

Applying through an education agent

Agents do not change what you pay. The fee schedule above is the schedule regardless of how your application reaches AGIT, and any agent charging you a fee is charging it for their own service, not for your course.

If you are unsure whether an agent is authorised, ask AGIT directly before you pay anyone anything.

Course length and breaks

A 52-week course is 40 weeks of scheduled study and 12 weeks of breaks. A 104-week course is 80 weeks of study and 24 weeks of breaks. All courses run a minimum of 20 scheduled classroom hours a week plus 4 hours of required self-directed study, which is the student visa condition.

Monthly intakes. An offer in one to five working days.

Apply directly, or through an authorised education agent. If you would rather ask a question first, that is the faster route — there is a real phone number and a real WhatsApp on the contact page.
