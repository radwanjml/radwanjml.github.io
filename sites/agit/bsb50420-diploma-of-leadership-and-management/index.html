Diploma of Leadership and Management

A year, twelve units, and the shortest route to a nationally recognised diploma at this campus. It is also the qualification that makes you eligible for the graduate diploma afterwards.

Code BSB50420 Duration 52 weeks Units 12 Delivery On campus Intakes Monthly

What it costs

Every amount includes the $750 material fee and the $250 non-refundable application fee. Onshore and offshore international students pay the same for this qualification.

If you areTuition Indicative total
An international student, onshore or offshore $10,500$11,500

One elective is doing more work than it looks

BSBMKG549, consumer behaviour for international markets, is an unusual elective in a general leadership diploma. For a cohort that is largely international, it is the unit most likely to transfer directly into work at home.

Getting in, and going on

Entry is at Year 12 level. This is the qualification most people at this campus start with.

Age
18 or over at enrolment.
English
IELTS Academic 6.0, PTE Academic 50 or TOEFL iBT 60, or a qualification delivered in English for at least twelve months, or an approved ELICOS program.
Prior study
Australian Year 12 or the overseas equivalent.
LLN
Assessed before enrolment, for everyone.
Licensing
No licensing, legislative or certification requirement applies to this qualification at the time of publication.
Leads to
BSB60420 Advanced Diploma of Leadership and Management, or a bachelor-level business or management degree at a CRICOS-approved higher education provider. It is also the entry qualification for BSB80120.
CRICOS course code
Confirm the course code with admissions.
