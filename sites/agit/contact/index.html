Who you will be talking to

Admissions handles applications, offers and fees. The same team handles agent enquiries. AGIT’s CRICOS student capacity is eighty, so this is a small operation — you will end up speaking to the same handful of people throughout.

A named student advisor and direct line are being published.

No human being is named anywhere on the current site

Not one. For a suspicious parent comparing five colleges, an institution with no named person is indistinguishable from a shell. The single highest-leverage change available here is a name, a photograph, a role and a direct WhatsApp — one person, on this page.

The CEO, Minaz Fatima Aleem, is already published by AGIT on both the CRICOS register and training.gov.au. She is named on the About page for that reason.

Send an enquiry

Six fields. Nothing here is required in order to see a fee — that is already on the fees page and it stays there.

Replies come from a person at the Jandakot campus, usually within one business day. If you need an answer faster, WhatsApp is quicker than email.

This form does not submit in the concept build

It is a working, keyboard-accessible, validating form with no endpoint behind it, because posting a stranger’s personal details to an inbox AGIT has not asked for would be wrong.

On a live build it posts to a serverless function that writes to admissions and returns a plain confirmation page — and it does not go live until a privacy policy exists, which is not yet published. Collecting personal data without one is a defect Tibr will not introduce.

Finding the campus

A two-storey rendered commercial building in a light-industrial estate at Monash Gate, off Armadale Road, about twenty kilometres south of the Perth CBD and close to Cockburn Central. There is a small red-and-white AGIT sign at the street edge.

Parking is on the street outside. If you are coming for the first time, call ahead — the estate has several units and the neighbours are parts distributors, not colleges.
