One IT qualification. Four specialisations.

Everything AGIT teaches in information technology sits under a single national code, ICT60220. The four streams below are elective choices inside that one qualification, not four separate courses.

Code ICT60220 Duration 104 weeks Units 16 Intakes Monthly Indicative $15,000–$27,000

What the four streams actually change

All four streams award the same qualification: the Advanced Diploma of Information Technology, ICT60220. All four run 104 weeks and require the same 16 units — 6 core and 10 elective. All four cost the same. All four have the same entry requirements.

What changes is which electives you take, and therefore what you spend most of your time doing. That is a real difference and it is worth choosing carefully. It is not four different diplomas.

The stream elective maps are not published

AGIT publishes one combined elective list for ICT60220 and does not publish which of those ten electives belongs to which stream. confirmed with admissions when you apply

Until that exists, the four streams below are described by what they are named and by the shared unit set, and no page here claims a stream-specific unit list that nobody has confirmed.

One unit title on AGIT’s printed flyer is wrong

The ICT60220 flyer prints ICTICT618 as “Manage AGIT, ethics and privacy in ICT environments”. The correct title, set by the national training package, is “Manage IP, ethics and privacy”. A find-and-replace on the brand acronym has overwritten a prescribed unit title. This page prints the correct one.

Getting in, and going on

Entry requirements are the same for all four streams.

Age
18 or over at the time of enrolment.
English
IELTS Academic 6.0, PTE Academic 50 or TOEFL iBT 60. A qualification delivered in English for at least twelve months is accepted instead, as is an approved ELICOS program.
Prior study
Australian Year 12 or the overseas equivalent.
Before you enrol
A language, literacy and numeracy assessment. Everyone sits it.
Where it leads
Bachelor programs in information technology, computer science, cyber security and networking at CRICOS-registered higher education providers.
CRICOS course code
Each registered course carries its own code, distinct from the provider code. Confirm this course’s code with admissions.

Monthly intakes. An offer in one to five working days.

Apply directly, or through an authorised education agent. If you would rather ask a question first, that is the faster route — there is a real phone number and a real WhatsApp on the contact page.
