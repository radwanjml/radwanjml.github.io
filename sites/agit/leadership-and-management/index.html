Two management qualifications, a year apart in level

One is a diploma you can start from Year 12. The other is postgraduate and needs a diploma before you begin. They are the same length and they are not alternatives to each other — they are consecutive.

Codes BSB50420 · BSB80120 Duration 52 weeks each Intakes Monthly Indicative $7,000–$14,500

One duration figure disagrees with itself

AGIT publishes BSB80120 as 52 weeks on the course flyer and the website, and as 104 weeks on the agent promotional flyer. The 52-week figure appears in three of four sources and is used here, marked as unconfirmed until AGIT says which is right in writing.

Both are delivered the same way

Face to face on campus, trainer-led, twenty scheduled hours a week plus four hours of required self-directed study. Assessment is competency-based — written responses, case studies, role-plays, presentations, projects and workplace-style documentation.

Monthly intakes. An offer in one to five working days.

Apply directly, or through an authorised education agent. If you would rather ask a question first, that is the faster route — there is a real phone number and a real WhatsApp on the contact page.
