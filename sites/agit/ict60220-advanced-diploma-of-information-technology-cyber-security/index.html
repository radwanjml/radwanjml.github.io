The EC-Council part

EC-Council issues the Certified Ethical Hacker credential. AGIT is a partner under its Academia Partnership Program.

For most people comparing cyber security courses, EC-Council is the one certification body whose name they already knew. The partnership gives AGIT students access to globally recognised certifications, industry-aligned curriculum and hands-on learning, and it applies to this specialisation and to all three tiers of the non-accredited AGIT IGNITE programme.

What the partnership does not say yet

Whether a certification exam voucher is included in the course fee, and which EC-Council certifications students are prepared for by name, is confirmed with admissions. This page will not imply a certification is included until it is confirmed that one is.

How you are assessed

Competency-based, against the requirements of each unit. There are no grades — you are either competent in a unit or you are not yet.

The methods are written knowledge questions, case studies, simulations, role-plays, practical demonstrations, presentations, portfolios, projects and workplace-style reports and documentation. Assessment is mapped to unit requirements and applies the principles of fairness, flexibility, validity and reliability.

What you need to supply yourself

A laptop with a current web browser, a valid email address, and the ability to produce .docx, .xlsx, .pptx and .pdf files. Reliable high-speed internet. Notebooks and stationery. A USB or external drive.

IT students specifically need additional storage capacity and the ability to install software, because a substantial part of this qualification runs in virtual machines.

Questions people actually ask

Is this a cyber security degree?

No. It is a nationally recognised advanced diploma — ICT60220 — in which cyber security is the elective specialisation. It is a pathway into a bachelor degree in IT, computer science, cyber security or networking at a CRICOS-registered higher education provider. It is not itself a degree, and anyone telling you otherwise is wrong.

How much does it cost, exactly?

Between $15,000 and $27,000 depending on your category, including the material and application fees. The exact figure is in your written Letter of Offer, and that document is the one that counts — not this page and not a flyer.

AGIT currently has four published fee sources that disagree with each other. That is explained in full on the fees page →

When does it start?

Intakes are monthly, subject to availability. A calendar of specific commencement dates is published each intake. Until it exists, confirm your date with admissions before you make travel or visa arrangements.

Does the course guarantee a job, or a visa?

No, and no page on this site will suggest it does. A qualification is a qualification. Student visas are decided by the Department of Home Affairs against their criteria, not by AGIT, and employment depends on the market you enter.

What if I have already studied some of this?

Apply for Recognition of Prior Learning or credit transfer. You can do it before you enrol or at any stage during study. Bring academic testimonials, statements of attainment, your CV and letters of reference from previous schools, RTOs or employers.

What if I do not finish?

You receive a Statement of Attainment for every unit you completed. The study you did is not lost, and it can be credited elsewhere.
