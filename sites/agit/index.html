An EC-Council Academia partner

EC-Council issues the Certified Ethical Hacker credential. If you are looking at cyber security, it is probably the one certification body whose name you already knew before you found this page.

What it means
AGIT teaches EC-Council’s industry-aligned curriculum under the Academia Partnership Program, with access to globally recognised certifications.
Where it applies
The Cyber Security specialisation of ICT60220, and all three tiers of the AGIT IGNITE short-course programme.
Also referenced
CompTIA, as a certification pathway on the short courses. Whether that is a formal partnership or a curriculum alignment is not yet confirmed.

Why there is no price here

AGIT advertises three IGNITE courses and publishes a fee for none of them — not on the website, not on a flyer, nowhere. This page will not invent one. Every empty slot on this site is drawn the way you see it above — visibly unfilled — so nothing plausible-looking ever ships in place of a number nobody has confirmed.

Five steps, and an offer in one to five working days

Apply directly or through an authorised education agent. The process is the same either way.

  1. Submit your application

    Online, or through an authorised agent, with your documents attached.

  2. Receive a Letter of Offer

    Course details, fees and conditions, issued in one to five working days. This document, not this website, is the authoritative statement of what you will pay.

  3. Accept and pay the initial amount

    Sign the Acceptance of Offer Agreement and pay the required initial tuition and fees, including the $250 non-refundable application fee.

  4. Get your Confirmation of Enrolment

    The CoE is what your student visa application is built on.

  5. Orientation, then class

    Orientation is mandatory. For some intakes it runs in the month before classes start.

One campus, in Jandakot

A two-storey commercial building in a light-industrial estate off Armadale Road, about twenty kilometres south of the Perth CBD and close to Cockburn Central.

It is not a university quadrangle and this site is not going to photograph it as one. It is a working campus with classrooms, student facilities and free Wi-Fi, on a quiet street with parking outside. Come and see it before you accept an offer if you can — and if you are offshore, ask for a video call instead.
