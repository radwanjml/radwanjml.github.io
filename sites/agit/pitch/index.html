Four fixes. Take them whether you hire us or not.

These are an afternoon of work on your existing WordPress site. They need no design decision, no new content and no budget. We are giving them to you because they are costing you enrolments this week.

  1. Add a title tag and meta description to the homepage

    Your highest-value page currently has neither. Google is guessing what to display, on the one page you most need it to get right. Ten minutes.

  2. Add Open Graph tags

    This is the one that matters most. Your site has no Open Graph tags at all, so every link an agent shares on WhatsApp renders as a blank grey box with a URL under it. For a student in Hyderabad who has never heard of you, that grey box is AGIT. Your primary distribution channel is currently showing nothing. Thirty minutes.

  3. Compress six oversized images

    Your homepage is roughly 14.7 MB. On throttled 4G in India or Nepal that is a 25 to 40 second load, which in practice means it does not load slowly — it fails. The same six images at the same visible quality come to about 1.5 MB. One afternoon.

  4. Point “Contact Us” at a contact page

    It currently resolves to /home/. Your highest-intent navigation item takes people nowhere. Two minutes.

What we found, worst first

Twenty findings, confirmed by direct inspection of the live site on 10 August 2026, and cross-checked against your four course flyers, your brand book, the CRICOS register and training.gov.au.

Losing you enrolments now

14.7 MB homepage
A 25–40 second load on 4G in your two largest source markets. Most of your target audience never sees the page render.
No Open Graph tags
Every agent’s WhatsApp share is a blank grey box. This is your primary recruitment channel and it is currently transmitting nothing.
Dead “Enroll” button
Two Enroll links exist; one points at #. Your primary conversion control does nothing.

Highest-intent nav item, nonsensical destination.

“View all courses” → #

No named human anywhere
Not one person is named on the entire site. For a parent deciding whether an overseas college is real, an institution with no named person is indistinguishable from a shell.

Costing you search traffic

No title, no meta description
On the homepage. Zero control over how your most valuable page appears in results.
Typo baked into a permalink
/ct60220-… is missing the i. That URL will never rank for the course code it is about.
Slug does not match content
Advanced Data Management lives at …it-strategy-organisational-development-2/.
Abandoned original
The -2 on /how-to-apply-2/ means something still occupies /how-to-apply/.
Agent journey collapsed
“Agents” and “Apply Now” both go to the same page. Agents are a primary channel and they have no route of their own.

Compliance exposure

Five fee rows do not add up
Three domestic ICT60220 rows state $18,500 tuition against a $15,000 total. Offshore BSB80120 parts sum to $14,500 against a stated $14,000. Two payment plans do not sum to their own totals. A prospective student cannot compute a correct figure from your published table, and published fee information carries obligations under the ESOS framework.
Four sources, four fee structures
Website, course flyers, the onshore/offshore flyer and the agent flyer each publish different numbers for the same qualifications. The spread on ICT60220 tuition is $16,000. All four are in circulation.
A prescribed unit title has been overwritten
ICTICT618 prints on your flyer as “Manage AGIT, ethics and privacy”. The national training package says “Manage IP”. A find-and-replace on your own acronym has corrupted a title you are not permitted to alter. This is the most serious documentation error we found.
Provider code in the course code field
Every flyer prints “CRICOS COURSE CODE: 04394M”. That is your provider code. Four qualifications cannot share one course code. This may be a labelling shortcut rather than a registration issue — but it needs resolving before any page publishes a course code, and we are asking rather than asserting.
One duration contradicts itself
BSB80120 is 52 weeks in three sources and 104 weeks on the agent flyer.

Undermining your credibility

RTO and CRICOS codes absent from the homepage
Registration is claimed in prose four times and never once evidenced with the number. The codes are the evidence; the prose is not.
A placeholder is live in production
blob (1) is sitting in your Industry Engagement carousel.
Your logo is a WhatsApp screenshot
Served as WhatsApp-Image-….jpeg, with the favicon derived from the same file — when a proper SVG of that logo already exists, because we drew it in October 2025.
Your brand book says three colours
Black, red, white. The live site uses blues, teals, photographic gradients and multi-colour icons. The identity you commissioned is not the identity you are running.
Three sections say the same thing
Consecutive homepage blocks restate the same four claims with no progression.
Your best credential is buried
The EC-Council Academia partnership is the strongest thing you have and it is inside a news carousel. EC-Council issues the CEH. It is the one certification body your cyber applicants already recognise.
