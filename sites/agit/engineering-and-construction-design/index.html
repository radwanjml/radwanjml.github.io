Civil construction design, from calculation to bill of quantities

One qualification in this faculty, and it is the most technical thing AGIT teaches: RII60520, the Advanced Diploma of Civil Construction Design.

Code RII60520 Duration 104 weeks Units 12 Intakes Monthly Indicative $15,000–$27,000

What you learn to actually do

The qualification is built around producing the documents a civil project runs on. Design analysis and calculations. Estimation. Surveying. Detailed drawings. Technical specifications. Bills of quantities.

Five of the twelve units are management rather than drafting — program risk, team effectiveness, safe design under work health and safety law, quality systems, negotiation. That is deliberate. Civil design work at this level is done inside a project structure, and the paperwork around a design is as much of the job as the design.

The printed flyer spells the qualification wrong

AGIT’s RII60520 flyer prints “CIVIL COUNSTRUCTION DESIGN”. The correct title is Advanced Diploma of Civil Construction Design, and it is what appears on this site and on the national register.
